Moving India Forward

LoadShift is reimagining commercial logistics — connecting businesses with verified vehicles and trusted drivers to power seamless, reliable transport across the nation.

Our Story

LoadShift was born in 2021 from a simple observation: India's commercial vehicle rental market was fragmented, opaque, and plagued by inefficiency. Small businesses struggled to find reliable transport, while thousands of truck owners sat idle between jobs. There had to be a better way.

Founded in Bengaluru by a team of logistics veterans and technologists, LoadShift set out to build the definitive platform for commercial vehicle rentals. We started with 40 vehicles in one city. Today, we operate a fleet of over 3,000 verified vehicles across 50+ cities, serving everyone from local retailers to Fortune 500 enterprises.

Our platform eliminates the middlemen, standardises pricing, and introduces accountability at every step — from booking to delivery. We believe that when logistics works, businesses thrive. And when businesses thrive, India moves forward.

What We Stand For

Our Values

The principles that guide every decision we make and every kilometre we cover.

Reliability

We show up — on time, every time. Our 99.2% on-time delivery rate isn't a marketing number; it's a promise backed by rigorous operations, real-time monitoring, and a deep respect for our clients' schedules.

Transparency

No hidden fees, no surprise surcharges, no fine print. Every quote on LoadShift is final. We believe trust is built through radical openness in pricing, tracking, and communication.

Innovation

From AI-powered route optimisation to real-time cargo condition monitoring, we invest relentlessly in technology that makes logistics smarter, faster, and more cost-effective for our customers.

Safety

Every driver is background-verified. Every vehicle is inspected quarterly. Full cargo insurance comes standard on every booking. We treat your goods with the same care we'd treat our own.
